## Outcome

Kelvin follows the board end to end:
alarm → collect data → rebuild incident → **quality agent** (usable / damaged / unsure → human QA) → find who pays (with a **claim plan** that can include a secondary payer) → prepare the case (**claim amount includes eligible extra costs**) → **manager approves, or rejects and the case closes** → send → follow up (**one appeal** on rejection or part-payment, **reminders** after 14 and 30 days of silence) → **record the result** (claimed vs recovered, recovery %, days to close, track record per payer).

## Decisions (from the board)

1. **Quality agent decides clear cases.** Compare peak °C and minutes out of 2–8 °C with the product's stability allowance. Within the allowance → usable → no claim, case closed with reasoning. Beyond the allowance, or no stability data → damaged → claim path. Within ±10% of either limit (configurable) → **unsure** → `awaiting_qa` for a human QA, never auto-closed. Reasoning is always logged (audit + record). A human QA can still decide while the case is `awaiting_qa`.
2. **Extra costs.** Loss = goods value + eligible extra costs (disposal, re-shipment, documented in the file). Each route's recoverable amount stays capped by its own limit (Montreal cap, contract cap, sum insured) minus deductible. Demo cases A/B/C get **no** extra costs, so their numbers don't move; new eval cases exercise it.
3. **Manager rejects a claim letter → case closed** with the reason logged: new status `closed_not_approved` (`awaiting_approval → closed_not_approved`). Rejecting a protective notice or another draft only rejects that draft. "Edit and approve" is unchanged.
4. **Follow-up.** On rejection or part-payment: **one** appeal with extra evidence (draft kind `reply`, `record.followUp.appeal`). After the appeal's answer, the manager decides via the existing settlement gate (accept partial or escalate). A second appeal is refused by a `ToolGuardError`. **Reminders** after 14 and 30 days without a reply (config `reminderDays: [14, 30]`), drafted as `reply` drafts that still need approval.
5. **Safety net.** The threshold stays 5 days left (stricter than "day 10 of 14"), and the protective notice is now also required while **required data is missing** (not only while QA is pending).
6. **Record the result.** On every closing transition, write `record.outcome`. The cases page shows a recovery rate, average days to close and a track record per payer.
7. **Claim plan.** Claim the party at fault first. When the recommended route is the insurer and the party in custody has a positive capped recovery, add a **secondary claim** against that party, up to the uninsured part (deductible + uninsured %). B: insurer €190,000 (primary) + airline €3,771 (secondary, towards the €10,000 deductible). C: forwarder €350,000, no secondary.
